Sites like LinkedIn and X are read in your Machine's own browser. Sometimes a site signs it out.

## Sign back in

The source's card, and the top of your Suggestions, say it's signed out. Press **Sign in again** and sign in, in a small window on the dashboard.

Tick **Save for next time** when you sign in. Next time the site logs your Machine out, it signs back in with one tap. The password stays encrypted on your Machine; the AI never sees it.

## Passwords and codes

Type passwords yourself. Two-step codes are yours to enter too.

Saved sign-ins are under **Connections**, **Sign-ins**. **Forget** removes one.
