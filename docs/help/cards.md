Your Machine reads everything new and makes a card only when something needs you. One card per matter, however many messages it spans.

## Where cards sit

The **Suggestions** tab groups them:

- **Ready for you**: prepared and waiting for your approval.
- **Needs your decision**: something only you can decide.
- **Preparing**: your Machine is working on it.
- **Coming back later**: snoozed.
- **Done / dismissed this week**.

**All clear.** means nothing needs you now.

## The buttons

A new card has **Do it**, **Mark done**, **Snooze**, **Change** and **Not needed**. Only a few show at once; the rest are under **More**, with **Rename**.

- **Do it**: your Machine prepares it. See [Do it](do-it.md).
- **Mark done**: it's handled.
- **Snooze**: bring it back later. See [Snooze](snooze.md).
- **Change**: tell it what you'd like instead, in a few words.
- **Not needed**: it goes away. Pick why, optionally: **Not relevant**, **Wrong facts** or **Already handled**. Your Machine learns from it.

A closed card can come back with **Reopen**.

## Make one yourself

**New card** (under **Find a card** on a computer) adds a card for anything. Creating it sends nothing.
