The dashboard installs as an app on your phone. It then sends a notification for each card, result and reminder, within your [work hours](work-hours.md).

## On an iPhone

1. Open your dashboard in Safari. iOS 16.4 or newer is needed.
2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
3. Open The Machine from your home screen, go to **Settings** and tap the bell next to **Phone notifications**.

Notifications only work from the home screen app.

## On Android

1. Open your dashboard in Chrome.
2. Open the menu and tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
3. Open it, go to **Settings** and tap the bell next to **Phone notifications**.

## More devices

Sign in on each device the same way. On a hosted Machine, a passkey per device makes signing in quicker: add one under Security on your account page.

To read your Android phone's notifications as a source, see [Android notifications](android.md).
