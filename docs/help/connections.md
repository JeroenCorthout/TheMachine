## How they're doing

The **Connections** tab groups your sources into **Mail and calendar**, **Chat** and **Other sources**. Each card says how it's doing: Connected, Waiting for first sync, Reconnecting automatically, or Needs your help. Open **Details** for **Recent syncs** and what to do.

## Add one

Open **+ Add** and pick a source. It asks what it needs, one question at a time. On a hosted Machine, Google and Microsoft link to your setup page. On your own computer, `assistant connect` does the same in a terminal.

## Work or personal

Each source counts as work (for a company you name) or personal. Change it under **Details**. Work cards wait for your work days.

## Remove one

Open the source, **Details**, then **Disconnect**. It stops reading. What it already brought in stays. On a hosted Machine, Google and Microsoft show **Reconnect or disconnect ↗**, which opens your setup page.

**Disconnected lately** lists Google, Microsoft and Zoom sign-ins you removed. **Removed, confirmed** means Google confirmed the sign-out, or for Microsoft and Zoom that no copy is left. **Pending** says why, and your Machine keeps trying.

## Saved sign-ins

Sites your Machine's browser uses, like LinkedIn or X, can save a sign-in: tick **Save for next time** when you sign in. Then **Sign-ins** lists them. When a site logs your Machine out, it signs back in with one tap. Passwords stay encrypted on your Machine. **Forget** removes one.

## Reviews

**Reviews** shows how your Machine is reading what comes in: recent runs, and what each one found. See [Reviews paused](reviews-paused.md).
