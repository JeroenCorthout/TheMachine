Your Machine notices promises in your messages, both ways, with nothing to set up.

## Where they are

Under **Promises** on the Suggestions tab:

- What you said you'd do, and for whom: **You promised**.
- What others said they'd do: **Promised to you**.

Each open one has **Done** and **Drop**. An open promise becomes a card when it's due, and shows on its day in the [Calendar](calendar.md).

On your own computer, `assistant promises` lists them too. The [weekly recap](weekly-recap.md) counts kept and open ones.
