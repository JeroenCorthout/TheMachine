## Snooze a card

Press **Snooze** on a card. **Remind me later** offers a few times, like in 2 hours, tomorrow, in 3 days or in a week, or **Choose date** for another day and **Time**, then **Save time**.

Snoozes of a day or more land at your "Snoozes land at" time, and work cards wait for a work day. Set both in [Work hours](work-hours.md).

A **Change** like "remind me about this next Tuesday" snoozes it too.

## While it's snoozed

The card sits under **Coming back later**, and on its day in the [Calendar](calendar.md). **Unsnooze** brings it back now, **Change time** picks another time.

On Outlook, the email thread moves to a Snoozed folder until the card comes back. See [Microsoft](microsoft.md).
