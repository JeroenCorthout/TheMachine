## Your Machine

- On your own computer: free. Everything The Machine does, with your own model.
- Hosted: {price.standard} a month for a Standard server, or {price.big} for a Bigger one, excl. VAT. The first {trial_days} days are free, once per person. Your card is needed to start, and nothing is charged before the free days end.

Your AI plan is billed by your provider, like OpenAI. We add nothing to it.

## On the Network

- Friends and Family: free, with any Machine.
- Work Fleet: {price.seat} a seat a month, or {price.seat_big} with a Bigger server, excl. VAT. A teammate who runs their own Machine is {price.network} a month, and one who's already hosted is free. Each Fleet has its own card and invoices, and the owner confirms every amount before it's charged.

All prices are per month, excl. VAT. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Card and invoices

On your account page, open Billing and press **Manage subscription**. It opens Stripe, where you change your card and download invoices. Your card details never reach us.

Change your server size any time from your account page.
