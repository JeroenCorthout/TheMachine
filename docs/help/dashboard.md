## The tabs

- **Suggestions**: your cards. See [Cards](cards.md).
- **Calendar**: the week ahead with your meetings, due cards and promises. See [Calendar](calendar.md).
- **Events**: everything your Machine has read, to search.
- **Graph**: the people, organisations and projects in your messages. See [Graph](graph.md).
- **Fleet**: your team, family and friends on The Machine Network, once you have one. See [Fleet](fleet.md).
- **Feedback**: how the cards are doing, and what it learned.
- **Connections**: your sources and how they're doing. See [Connections](connections.md).

**Settings** is the gear at the top. **Find a card** searches your cards by title.

## Events

Search everything that came in, by meaning or by words, across all accounts or one. Each result opens in a reader, with a link to open it where it came from.

## Feedback

- **How the cards are doing**: over the last 30 days, how many were **Worth it**, done on the **First try**, **Finished**, and **Sent as is**.
- **What I've learned**: how you write, facts and how you like things done, from your notes. **Edit** any of it in your own words, and **Remove** anything that's no longer true. When a note on a card teaches a fact or rule, the card's chat says so under the reply (**Saved to what I’ve learned**, with **Edit** and **Undo**); that line comes from what was stored, so a reply never just claims it.
- **What you decided**: a timeline of your choices on cards.
