A hosted Machine runs on a private server of its own in the EU. We keep it running and updated.

## Sign up

1. At app.themachine.computer, press **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**. This makes your account.
2. Scan the code with an authenticator app, type the six digits and press **Turn on**. A passkey can come later.
3. Pick a server size and your Machine's name, then add your card. The first {trial_days} days are free and nothing is charged before they end.

## Your setup page

Your server starts, then the setup page walks you through the rest. Each step is a card:

1. **Your model**: sign in with ChatGPT or use your own key. See [ChatGPT](chatgpt.md).
2. **Mail and Calendar**: connect Google or Microsoft. See [Gmail](gmail.md) and [Microsoft](microsoft.md).
3. Reviews turn on by themselves. First cards come from your newest mail, then from your last 7 days.
4. Optional: [Telegram](telegram.md), and **More sources** such as Slack, WhatsApp or GitHub.

When it's ready, press **Open your dashboard**. Add it to your phone's home screen for notifications: see [The phone app](phone-app.md).

## Come back later

Your setup page stays at app.themachine.computer/setup. Add or remove sources there any time. In the dashboard, **Settings** has **Open account**.
