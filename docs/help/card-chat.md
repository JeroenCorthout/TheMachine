## Open it

On a phone, tap the card's title. On a computer, use the card's chat link or **See in chat ›**. On a computer, clicking the title renames the card.

## What to ask

- About the thread, the people or the background: "What did they ask last time?"
- To change the draft: "Make it shorter" or "Mention the new date".

A question gets an answer and leaves the draft as it is. Approvals stay on the card's buttons: chat never sends a draft in the same reply that wrote it.

Files can't be added in chat.
