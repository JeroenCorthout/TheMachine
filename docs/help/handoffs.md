## Hand off a card

1. On the card, pick **Hand off to a teammate**.
2. Choose who, check what goes along (leave any part out with ×), and add a note.
3. Send it. Their Machine shows it to them as a card.

While you wait, the card sits under **Coming back later** with a check-in date. **Check in now** asks how it's going; **Change time** moves the check-in.

## When they decline

They can say why: not theirs, already done, or no time this week. Then pick **Do it myself**, **Hand to someone else** or **Mark done**.

## Someone outside the Network

Pick **By email**: your Machine drafts an email for you to send. Add a task page so they can answer in one tap: see [Task pages](task-pages.md).
