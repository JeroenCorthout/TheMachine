## What it shows

The next 7 days, with:

- meetings from the calendars you connected,
- cards due that day, and snoozed cards on the day they come back,
- check-ins on work you handed off,
- promises with a date.

A meeting on two calendars shows once. A calendar someone shared with you shows their events muted, with their first name; those never make a card. All-day and multi-day events show as all day, on each day they cover.

A plan made together is one promise, marked "With" and their name. A promise about a meeting that day with the same person sits on that meeting's row.

**Overdue** comes first. A meeting with an open card shows it: **Prep ready**, **Preparing** or **Card**.

## Agenda or week

**Agenda** lists each day. On a computer, **Week** shows a grid. Your choice is remembered on that device.

## Open something

Click a row to open its card's chat, the promise, or the meeting in its own calendar. Looking changes nothing.

## Add or change an event

When a calendar can be changed from here, **New event** adds one: pick the calendar, a title and the time. Click an event without guests on that calendar to change it, or **Delete** it (tap twice). Nobody is invited or gets an update.

A card can offer a change too, like a pick-up to add. It shows exactly what changes, and only your **Add**, **Save** or **Delete** makes it.
