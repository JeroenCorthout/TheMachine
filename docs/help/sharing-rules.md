## Set them

In the **Fleet** tab, open Sharing. Each kind of question has a rule:

- Ask me first: you see each question and decide.
- Always: your Machine answers by itself.
- Never: it declines.
- Names: only names, no details.

It never shares email content, or what your meetings are about. "Different for someone? Add an exception" sets a rule for one person.

Rules change only on the dashboard, never from a message.

## The first time someone asks

A question that needs you comes as a card, with **Share once**, **Always share**, **Decline** or **Never share**.
