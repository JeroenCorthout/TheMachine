A skill tells your Machine how to do something, like "answer pricing questions". It's instructions only: a skill never changes what your Machine may do.

## Write one

In **Settings**, **Skills**, press **New skill** or **Start from an example**. Give it a **Name**, **When to use it** and the **Instructions**, then **Save**.

## Import

Press **Import**:

- **From Claude** or **From ChatGPT**, then **Find my skills**. It looks in that account, in your Machine's browser.
- Or bring one yourself: **Upload .skill or .zip**, **Upload a folder** or **Paste a SKILL.md**.

Pick the ones you want and press **Import selected**. Only instructions come in; scripts never do.

## Search ClawHub

Skills other people publish. They're third-party: read one before you install it.

## Share one

Share a skill with a friend or teammate. They read it and choose to install it. Later edits go out only if you share again.

More in the [skills guide](https://github.com/Salesflare/TheMachine/blob/main/docs/skills.md).
