## Cancel

1. On your account page, open Billing and press **Cancel subscription**.
2. Confirm. It ends with the month you paid for. Cancel in the free days and you pay nothing.

Changed your mind? **Keep my subscription** takes it back. In the EU you can also withdraw within 14 days with **Withdraw from contract here**.

## Your data after you cancel

It's kept for {grace_days} days so you can export it, then deleted, backups included. To keep using it on your own computer, see [Move to your own computer](moving.md).

## Delete everything now

On your account page, open Data, type DELETE and press **Wipe everything now**. It ends your subscription, revokes Google and Microsoft access, and deletes your server, its backups and all its data. This can't be undone.

Without a hosted server, the button is **Delete my account**. A Machine on your own computer stays as it is.
