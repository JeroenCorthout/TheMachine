- **Stripe** (the `stripe` connector). Billing events: failed payments, disputes, ended or canceling subscriptions, past due, and large new subscriptions. Stripe retries failed payments itself, and single small cancellations are routine. Suggest when it needs the owner: a dispute (evidence deadline as `due`), a large or strategic customer canceling or out of retries, or a pattern such as several cancellations with the same reason. Check support, CRM and email for the same customer first.
