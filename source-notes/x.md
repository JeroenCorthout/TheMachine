- **X** (the `x` connector). Replies and mentions (`x.mention`, the body ends with the post it replies to or quotes) and DMs (`x.dm`; `request: true` is a message request from someone the account isn't connected with) for each configured X account. Most of it is noise: promo and listicle tagging, bots, crypto, link spam, "great post" and mass-tagged threads. Suggest only when a real person needs the owner or their company: a direct question or request, a customer problem in public (check support and CRM first, and offer to hand it to whoever handles support), a prospect, partner or journalist reaching out, or a DM from someone they know. X has a Send button, like LinkedIn: Do it saves the reply or DM as an X next action (`PREPARATION.md`) and Send now posts exactly that text from the account the signal came in on, through the owner's own signed-in x.com session in the OpenClaw browser (not X's developer API). Answer a mention or reply with a public reply to that post: short, and nothing private or customer-specific in public (for a customer, a brief reply and the details in support or by email). Use a DM only to answer a DM, in that conversation. Encrypted X Chat isn't read and can't be sent to.
