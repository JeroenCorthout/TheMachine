- **Calendar.** Routine instances of placeholder or routine recurring series (Lunch, Block, Home, Office, Stand up and similar; see README) aren't collected. Other recurring meetings, one-off meetings, moved instances and cancellations are. Calendar is context for email and preparing actions; don't send routine appointment reminders. An event missing from a complete scan is "unavailable", not necessarily canceled. Keep original dates and time zones, and don't infer attendance from an invitation. Titles containing `'s birthday` are excluded only when that account's `excluded_title_terms` config includes it; do not invent owner-specific filters. Don't start new OAuth flows without a concrete need.
